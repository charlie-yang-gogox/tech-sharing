At the bottom of the distribution, 12 tickets came in under 1.5×, all of them 5–9-session hard problems. AI does not rescue an under-scoped hard problem.

08 / FLOW

瓶頸不在寫，在等人審The constraint is not writing — it is waiting for review

98.1%
下一個數量級的增益來自 review 容量，不是更快的模型。AI 沒有讓 review 變快 — 它讓更多工作湧到 review 前面。 The next order-of-magnitude gain is review capacity, not a faster model. AI did not make review faster — it made more work arrive at review.
講稿備註 · 這頁對老闆最有價值（不上投影片）SPEAKER NOTES · the slide with the most value to a manager (not projected)

這頁是在替下一季要資源鋪路This slide is the ask, one slide early — 不要講成抱怨。講成：我們已經把「寫」這一段解決了，現在唯一的槓桿在「審」。Do not frame it as a complaint. Frame it as: we have solved the writing half; the only remaining lever is the reviewing half.

如果被問「那 AI 是不是反而更慢」If asked whether AI made things slower — 誠實答：AI 的 PR 中位數 44.3 小時才合併，人的是 22.8 小時。原因是 AI 的 PR 更大（異動 419 行 vs 265 行），排在同一批 reviewer 後面。這不是 AI 慢，是產能超過了審查容量。Answer honestly: AI PRs merge in 44.3 h median against 22.8 h for human ones, because they are larger (419 vs 265 changed lines) and queue behind the same reviewers. That is not AI being slow; that is output outrunning review capacity.

10 / SO WHAT

三件該做的事Three things to do next

依槓桿排序，每一條的理由都是前面某一頁量到的東西，不是意見。 Ranked by leverage. Each reason is something measured on an earlier slide, not an opinion.

01

大票交給 AI，小票別給Give AI the big tickets, not the trivial ones
1 點的票只有 1.8×，3 點的票 9.3×。前置成本是固定的，小票等於白付。 1-point tickets return 1.8×; 3-point tickets return 9.3×. The setup cost is fixed, so small tickets spend it for almost nothing.

02

擴 review 容量Add review capacity
98% 的前置時間在 review 佇列。把 AI 的建置時間砍一半，只換到不到 1% 的改善。 98% of lead time is the review queue. Halving AI build time buys under 1%.

03

已上線的 CAF 票只有 30% 有 session 資料。速度那兩頁都建立在這個樣本上，補起來很便宜。 Only 30% of shipped CAF tickets carry session data. The speed slides rest on that sample; widening it is cheap.
